# Terms

Kept source code is MIT licensed. It supports customer-promise capture, planning
assessment, human-reviewed follow-up and evidence retention. A planning state is
not a delivery probability, contractual interpretation or guarantee.

A person confirms what was promised, the customer organisation, agreed date,
timezone, owner and required work. Customer availability and amendments require
their own evidence. Source labels and URLs record provenance; Kept does not
authenticate the underlying document or replace the organisation's approval policy.

All customer communication uses the organisation's own channel after review.
The app's Rovo and MCP tools are read-only. Customer delivery is distinct from
Jira task completion.

The synthetic replay is for evaluation. Atlassian platform, billing, Rovo and
connector terms apply to installed versions. A public replay does not establish
the live Forge adapter's production readiness.
