Privacy

Kept's Forge adapter stores customer organisation identifiers and names, optional renewal dates, source reply snapshots, promise dates, linked Jira keys, account IDs, delivery evidence and revision history in the installing site's app storage. It uses those records to assess planning risk and retain reviewed decisions.

Reads require access to the source issue and every linked delivery item. Writes also require Edit Issues on the source request. Source comments are re-read as the current user and must be public for capture. A public JSM reply may still contain personal or commercially sensitive information; use the site's approved data policy and record only the excerpt and source needed for the promise.

Background events read current Jira data as the app. They record internal owner alerts. Viewing alerts requires the same source-and-work permissions. They do not send email, post support replies or call an external model endpoint.

Graph ingestion is restricted in code to explicitly synthetic demonstration documents. Those documents are visible to the workspace after an administrator connects the graph. Real customer promise text, account IDs, renewal dates and source reply bodies are not eligible for this projection. Disconnecting removes the connector's graph data; Forge evidence retention is separate.

The standalone replay uses fictional organisations and people. Its records remain in the visitor's browser. Reset replay removes local edits. No analytics or remote storage is used. Exported evidence files are under the downloading user's control.

The installing organisation determines lawful retention, access, source correction, backup, deletion and app removal. Production deployment and retention controls require organisational review. Contact the repository maintainer through the project's GitHub issue tracker for support; do not include sensitive records in a public issue.
